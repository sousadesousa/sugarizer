Admin dashboard

Sugarizer Server comes with a dashboard for teachers and for whoever runs the server. It manages users and classrooms, chooses which activities appear on the home screen, and shows statistics. It is opened in a browser at /dashboard on the server. Choose a page below.

    Notes and limits

    • Ages and categories are my suggestions, not Sugarizer's. They come from each activity's own welcome text and from opening it, and I could not test with children.
    • Screenshots show the start screen, except Paint, Physics JS and Get Things Done, where I added a little content.
    • 3D Volume, Human Body and Gears appear empty: the 3D scenes did not draw in my test browser and no gears appeared when I tried to add some, so I could not confirm those three beyond their start screen.
    • Dollar Street, Video Viewer and Ebook Reader show an offline message without the Internet, Chat needs a Sugarizer Server, and Record and Measure need a camera or microphone.
    • Jappy shows an error while a new instance starts, and Constellation sometimes shows a script error while loading.
    • 3D Volume, Exerciser, Gears, Get Things Done, Markdown, MediaViewer, Pomodoro and Stickman have no welcome text of their own, so I described them from their toolbar and start screen.
    • The dashboard screenshots come from a test server with sample students. Its Journals and Assignments pages are empty because I could not add entries to the test database.
